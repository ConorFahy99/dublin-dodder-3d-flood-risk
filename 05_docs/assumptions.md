# Assumptions

To be filled in as the analysis develops. Expected entries:

- **Floor-to-floor height** — value used to convert flood depth to storeys inundated, with justification.
- **Water surface method** — how the flood water surface elevation was derived (OPW depth grid directly, or DTM plus modelled level).
- **Building height source** — nDSM (DSM minus DTM) or OSM `building:levels`, and how gaps were filled.
- **Catchment extent** — how the study area boundary was defined.
